How do I switch visual themes?

Tap the THEMES button on the Home Screen or inside the in-game PAUSED menu. Sweet Dessert is active by default; Wood & Acrylic unlocks at Level 10 and Minimalist Nordic unlocks at Level 20 (or unlock either early for free by watching a short rewarded ad).

How do I mute sound or turn off haptics?

You can toggle SFX and Haptics anytime from the Home Screen Settings gear icon, or directly inside the in-game PAUSED menu.

How do I manage my Ad Privacy settings?

Open Settings → AD PRIVACY & POLICY inside the game to review or update your consent preferences, or read our full Privacy Policy.

How do I delete my game data?

All progress is stored locally on your device. Simply uninstalling the app or clearing app storage in your device settings permanently removes all local data.
