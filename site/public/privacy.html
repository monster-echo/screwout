Privacy Policy

Effective Date: November 26, 2026 · App: Screw Out: Sweet Jam (com.screwout.sweetjam)
Developer: Suzhou Zhongbei Technology Co., Ltd.

1. Overview & Scope

This Privacy Policy explains how Suzhou Zhongbei Technology Co., Ltd. ("we", "our", or "us") handles information when you play Screw Out: Sweet Jam on iOS (Apple App Store) and Android (Google Play). The game works completely without an account. We do not ask for or collect your name, email address, phone number, contacts, photos, microphone, or precise GPS location.

2. Data Stored Locally on Your Device

Your level progress, unlocked themes, booster counts, 3D pixel artwork progress, sound, and haptic preferences are stored locally on your device using platform local storage. We do not upload your gameplay save to our own servers.

3. Advertising & Third-Party SDKs (Google AdMob)

Screw Out: Sweet Jam is free to play and supported by rewarded video ads and interstitial ads provided by Google AdMob (Google LLC).

  • Data processed by Google AdMob: Depending on your region and consent choices, Google AdMob may process device identifiers (such as IDFA on iOS or Advertising ID on Android), IP address, coarse diagnostic/crash metrics, and ad interaction data (impressions, clicks) to serve ads, cap ad frequency, prevent fraud, and measure ad performance.
  • GDPR / EEA / UK & US State Privacy Consent (Google UMP): Before requesting ads in applicable regions, we present Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP) consent form. You can review or change your ad privacy choices at any time inside the game via Settings → AD PRIVACY & POLICY.
  • iOS App Tracking Transparency (ATT): On iOS 14.5+, we request your permission via Apple's ATT prompt before accessing the Identifier for Advertisers (IDFA). If you select "Ask App Not to Track", we respect your choice and only non-personalized / non-tracked ad requests are made.
  • Learn more about how Google uses data in its Google Privacy Policy and How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services.

4. Data Sharing & Security

We do not sell your personal data. Data is processed only by the platform providers required to operate and distribute the game: Google AdMob (advertising & fraud prevention) and Apple / Google (app store distribution and system crash diagnostics). All network requests use HTTPS encryption.

5. Data Retention & Deletion

Because game progress is stored solely on your device, you can delete all local game data at any time by clearing app storage or uninstalling Screw Out: Sweet Jam. You may also reset or limit your Advertising ID in your iOS or Android system settings.

6. Children's Privacy

Screw Out: Sweet Jam is designed for a general audience and is not directed to children under 13 (or the minimum digital consent age in your jurisdiction). We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal data, please contact us and we will promptly address it.

7. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect app updates or legal requirements. The updated version will be posted on this page with a revised effective date.
